Step 1: Export what Canva will actually give you

Canva Pro lets you download designs as PNG, SVG, PDF Print, and MP4, and lets you export a Brand Kit inventory (logos, color hex codes, uploaded fonts-as-files list). What you cannot export is the editable layered project itself in a format Adobe Express can open. So the migration unit is assets, not projects:

  • Export every evergreen template as PDF Print and SVG pair (PDF for print fidelity, SVG for re-editable vectors).
  • Download all brand kit assets (logos, textures, licensed fonts you own).
  • Re-download paid stock usage records — Canva's licensed stock does not transfer, so note which images must be replaced or re-licensed in Adobe Stock.

Step 2: Rebuild the brand kit in Adobe Express

Adobe Express rebuilds the same concept as Brand Kit: logo, colors, fonts. Paste your hex codes, upload the font files you legitimately own, and re-create your logo lockups as templates. Expect a half-day for a typical small brand — this is mostly transcription, not redesign.

Step 3: Rebuild your top 5 templates, not all of them

Migrate the five designs that drive 80% of your output (usually social post, story, banner, pitch slide, and one print piece). Recreate them once as Adobe Express templates, share them to your team, and let new designs inherit from them. Migrating the full Canva folder structure is the classic way to stall a switch for weeks.

Step 4: Run one overlap month

Adobe Express has a free tier and a trial, so run both tools side by side for 30 days. Rebuild live projects in Express only, keep Canva read-only for reference, and cancel Canva before its next renewal date so you don't pay for a phantom month.

FAQ

Q: Can Adobe Express open my Canva designs?

A: No. Export Canva designs as SVG (vectors) or PDF Print and rebuild the template in Express — one-time work that takes 10-20 minutes per template.

Q: Do my Canva stock images carry over?

A: No. Canva's stock licenses don't transfer. Re-source equivalents from Adobe Stock or keep one read-only Canva seat for reference.

Q: Is Adobe Express enough if I also own Photoshop?

A: That's the sweet spot — quick social assets in Express, deep edits handed off to Photoshop/Illustrator, which Canva has never matched natively.
