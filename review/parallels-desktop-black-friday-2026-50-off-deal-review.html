What the Parallels Black Friday 2026 deal actually gets you

Parallels has a predictable rhythm. The annual release lands in August, the price stays fixed all year, and then Black Friday week the storefront starts discounting first-year subscriptions — usually starting the Monday before Thanksgiving and running through Cyber Monday. Cyber Monday itself is frequently the lowest price of the window, but only by a few dollars, and the popular editions have sold out of promo codes before (yes, promo code pools run dry).

Two things worth knowing before you click anything:

The 50% tier is usually the Pro edition or a first-year-only rate. The Standard edition more often sits in the 30–40% range during Black Friday. If you see "50% off" and it turns out to be 50% off the Pro upgrade path, that's not a scam, it's just marketing doing marketing things. Check which edition the number applies to before you get excited.

Subscriptions include major version upgrades; perpetual licenses don't. A subscription bought this week covers you through Parallels Desktop 28 next August. A perpetual license locks you to the version you bought, plus minor point updates. On a Black Friday price, the subscription is almost always the better math — a discounted year costs about the same as a discounted perpetual license, and you get the next release thrown in.

Parallels also runs a 14-day free trial, and the company advertises a 30-day money-back guarantee on direct purchases. Confirm both at checkout, but that combination means you can test your specific Windows app on your specific Mac before you're committed.
