Your calendar is a battlefield. Back-to-back Zoom calls, Google Meets, Teams meetings – and someone still has to take notes. For years, that someone was you, scribbling furiously while the conversation moved on. But by 2026, AI note takers have become the default for anyone who values their time.

Three names keep coming up: Otter.ai, Fireflies.ai, and Fathom. I’ve used all three daily for weeks, on real calls with clients, team standups, and even a few chaotic brainstorming sessions. Here’s the honest breakdown – no fluff, no hype, just what each tool actually delivers.

What Each Tool Does Best (in 10 Seconds)

  • Otter.ai – The original. Lives in your browser and mobile, built for real-time transcription and collaborative notes. Best if you want a shared workspace for meeting notes.
  • Fireflies.ai – The Swiss Army knife. Records from 50+ platforms (Zoom, Teams, Google Meet, Webex, you name it). Heavy on search and action items.
  • Fathom – The minimalist. No transcription clutter – gives you clean summaries, highlights, and action items. Best for people who hate reading transcripts.

Feature Showdown: What You Actually Care About

Transcription Accuracy

I tested each tool on a 45-minute recorded call with three speakers (two Americans, one British), moderate background noise, and a few technical terms like “REST API” and “asynchronous.”

  • Otter.ai: ~95% accuracy. Handled the British accent well, stumbled on “asynchronous” (wrote “a-sync-ronous”). Speaker labels are automatic and mostly correct if you upload a calendar or invite list.
  • Fireflies: ~93% accuracy. Struggled a bit with overlapping speech – it tagged the wrong speaker a few times. But it corrected terms like “REST API” perfectly after I trained it with custom vocabulary.
  • Fathom: Doesn't give you a full transcript by default. That’s by design – it generates summaries and timestamps snippets. For the raw text accuracy test, I had to export the transcript manually. Result: ~96% accuracy for the summary snippets, but the full transcript was gated behind a Pro plan.

Winner: Otter for pure transcription. Fathom if you care more about summaries than word-for-word capture.

Summaries & Action Items

This is where the tools really differ.

  • Otter.ai offers “AI Chat” – you can ask questions like “What were the three main decisions?” It returns decent summaries, but they’re often too long (think 800-word paragraphs). Action items are pulled automatically, but they’re sometimes buried in the notes.
  • Fireflies has a “Soundbites” feature that highlights key moments and a “Smart Search” that lets you find any word spoken in any meeting. Its automatic summary is a short paragraph with bulleted action items – clear and useful. You can also ask Fred (their AI) for follow-up emails.
  • Fathom wins on simplicity. After each call, you get a clean summary (3-5 bullet points), a list of action items with assignees, and highlighted moments you can click to jump to that part of the recording. No clutter. No “view full transcript” unless you want it.

Winner: Fathom for the cleanest summaries. Fireflies for searchability.

Recording and Bot Behavior

  • Otter.ai: Can join meetings via a bot (Otter Assistant) that sends a link to the transcript. Works on Zoom, Google Meet, and Teams (via calendar). The bot announces itself (“Otter is recording this meeting…”). No stealth recording.
  • Fireflies: Fred, the bot, joins the meeting and can be set to record without audio notification (other participants see a “Recording” indicator). It’s the most flexible for recording without disrupting flow – but check your local laws on consent.
  • Fathom: The bot joins and announces itself briefly. It doesn’t record video – only audio + screen share. That’s a privacy plus for some teams.

Pros and Cons

Otter.ai

Pros

  • Excellent real-time transcription – you can follow along live
  • AI Chat lets you ask specific questions about past meetings
  • Mobile app is fully functional for recording in-person chats
  • Strong collaboration features: comments, highlights, shared folders

Cons

  • Summaries are too verbose – not good for quick scanning
  • Limits meeting length on Free plan to 30 minutes (annoying for long strategy calls)
  • Bot announces itself (no stealth mode) – can be distracting
  • Search across meetings is decent but not as powerful as Fireflies

Fireflies.ai

Pros

  • Wildest integration list – connects to almost everything
  • Soundbites let you share memorable 30-second clips (great for client approvals)
  • Smart Search (find any word in any meeting, even across multiple recordings)
  • Custom vocabulary training improves accuracy for jargon-heavy industries

Cons

  • The UI can feel bloated – too many tabs and options
  • Speaker identification struggles with overlapping speech more than Otter
  • Free plan storage is limited (800 minutes) – you’ll hit it fast
  • Some users report the bot causing minor audio issues in certain Zoom rooms

Fathom

Pros

  • Completely free for individual use – no catch
  • Cleanest summaries in the game – bullet points, no fluff
  • Action items with assignees are automatically suggested
  • Frictionless setup: install the Chrome extension, join a meeting, get a summary
  • No video recording (privacy-friendly)

Cons

  • No mobile app – you need a computer to view notes
  • No real-time transcription – you can’t follow along during the meeting
  • Limited integrations compared to Fireflies (though core ones are covered)
  • Full transcript is gated behind Pro (though most users won’t need it)

Use Cases: Which Tool for Which Situation?

You’re a freelance consultant who records client calls?

Go with Fathom. It’s free, produces clean summaries you can copy-paste into your CRM, and clients won’t be annoyed by a bot that says “recording.”

You’re a sales team that needs to search through thousands of calls for a specific objection?

Choose Fireflies. The search is phenomenal – you can type “pricing objection” and get every quote across all your reps’ calls.

You’re a product manager running daily standups and want a shared note-taking area?

Stick with Otter.ai. The real-time collaboration (everyone can view, comment, and highlight live) is unique. It’s like having a live transcript that becomes the team’s permanent record.

Final Verdict: Which AI Meeting Note Taker Should You Use in 2026?

If you’re a solo operator or a small team, Fathom is the obvious winner. It’s free, it’s fast, and it gives you exactly what you need: a clean summary with action items. No extra fluff. No need to upgrade unless you grow.

If you’re a power user who needs to search across hundreds of meetings and integrate with your entire tech stack, Fireflies is the best investment. The $19/month Pro plan pays for itself when you’re saving an hour a week hunting for meeting details.

If you value live collaboration – especially in team environments where everyone wants to see the transcript as it happens – Otter.ai remains the king of real-time transcription. But for most people, the summaries are too long and the free plan is too restrictive.

My pick: For 2026, I’m using Fathom for all my client calls and Fireflies for team-wide recording. Otter is the legacy choice – still great, but the competition has caught up.

FAQ

Can I use these tools on my personal meeting links without the bot joining?

Otter and Fathom require the bot to join (they announce themselves). Fireflies can be set to record without an audio announcement, but it still appears as a participant. No stealth bot that’s completely invisible.

Do they work on Microsoft Teams?

All three work, but Otter and Fathom rely on calendar sync (they need the meeting link). Fireflies has a dedicated Teams app integration that works more reliably.

Which one has the best search?

Fireflies, by a mile. You can search by keyword, date, speaker, and even sentiment. Otter has basic search. Fathom has search within a meeting’s summary but not across all meetings unless you upgrade.

Are they GDPR compliant?

Otter and Fireflies offer data residency options on Business plans. Fathom processes data in the US. Check your compliance requirements if you’re in Europe.

Can I get a refund if I don’t like it?

Otter offers 30-day refunds on annual plans. Fireflies offers 7-day no-questions-asked refunds. Fathom is free, so no refund needed.

Bottom line: Stop taking manual notes. Pick Fathom if you want free and clean. Pick Fireflies if you want power. Pick Otter if you need real-time collaboration.
