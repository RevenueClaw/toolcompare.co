Surfshark vs NordVPN on Black Friday 2026: Two-Year Pricing Compared Honestly

Every November, the two biggest names in VPNs launch discounts that sound almost identical: "up to 86% off," "extra months free," "limited time." Then you get to the checkout page and the numbers look nothing like the banner.

So let's do this properly. Below is a side-by-side of what Surfshark and NordVPN actually charge on two-year plans during Black Friday 2026, what those plans renew at, and where each one genuinely beats the other. No "unbeatable" claims. Just the maths, the trade-offs, and a recommendation at the end.

One thing worth knowing before we start: Surfshark and NordVPN are owned by the same parent company. Nord Security acquired Surfshark in 2022. They still operate as separate brands with separate apps, separate server networks and separate pricing — and they still compete hard for your Black Friday dollar. But it does explain why their privacy policies, audit standards and general approach to no-logs logging look so similar.

The short version

  • Cheapest two-year deal: Surfshark, at roughly $1.99/month for the VPN-only tier.
  • Best VPN for the money: NordVPN, at roughly $2.99–$3.59/month. You pay about $1/month more for a bigger network, faster long-distance speeds and stronger built-in protections.
  • Biggest gotcha for both: renewal pricing. Neither brand renews at the Black Friday rate.
  • Device limits: Surfshark is unlimited. NordVPN is 10.

What Black Friday actually changes (and what it doesn't)

Here's the honest mechanics of a VPN Black Friday sale, because this is where most people get burned.

Neither company discounts the monthly plan much. The deals are almost entirely on 24-month prepaid terms, sometimes with two to four bonus months tacked on. You pay the whole thing upfront. In exchange, the per-month rate drops by 70–85% versus monthly billing.

Two things stay the same no matter how good the banner looks:

  • The renewal price is not the promo price. When your two years end, you'll be billed at the standard 24-month rate, which is meaningfully higher. Both companies disclose this, both bury it a few clicks deep.
  • The 30-day money-back guarantee is real. This is the single most useful thing about buying a VPN during Black Friday. You can test the full product for a month and get a full refund if it doesn't perform on your connection.

Feature breakdown

Server network

Surfshark runs around 3,200 servers across 100 countries. NordVPN runs 6,400+ across 111 countries, with a much deeper presence in South America, Africa and Southeast Asia.

In practice: for US, UK and Western Europe, both are fine. If you travel to or do business in places like Brazil, Nigeria, Vietnam or Turkey, NordVPN's extra coverage shows up as better speeds and less congestion. Surfshark has improved here, but the network is roughly half the size.

Speed

Both use WireGuard-based protocols — Surfshark calls it WireGuard, NordVPN calls it NordLynx, which adds a double NAT layer for privacy.

On a 1 Gbps home connection with nearby servers, both regularly push 700–900 Mbps. The difference appears over distance. Surfshark is not slow by any measure — it's just consistently a step behind on long-haul routes.

If you mostly connect to servers in your own country (streaming, banking, working from a café), you won't notice the difference. If you're routing from Asia to Europe daily, you will.

Streaming and unblocking

Both unblock Netflix, BBC iPlayer, Disney+, Amazon Prime Video, Hulu and the rest. Both have dedicated server categories for it.

Surfshark's advantage: unlimited devices means you can spread one subscription across a whole household, including a router, without counting connections. NordVPN's 10-device cap is generous for a person, tight for a family of five with phones, laptops, tablets and a TV.

NordVPN's advantage: slightly better consistency on regional libraries that rotate their blocking more aggressively, and a SmartDNS feature for devices that don't support VPN apps.
